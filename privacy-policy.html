Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

SafeTay Classic Restoration ("SafeTay", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. It's written to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

SafeTay Classic Restoration is a classic car restoration and detailing business based in Blairgowrie, Scotland, United Kingdom.

Registered/trading address: [full business address to be added here].

If you have any question about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at: info@safetay.uk. As a small business, we don't have a dedicated Data Protection Officer — enquiries are handled directly by the business owner, who acts as our data protection contact.

2. What data we collect

We only collect what we need to run the business and respond to you. Depending on how you interact with the site, that can include:

  • Contact and enquiry details you type into our consultation or contact forms: your name, phone number, email address, and any notes about your vehicle or project.
  • Photos of your vehicle, if you choose to attach any when submitting a consultation or contact form. Attaching photos is entirely optional.
  • Correspondence you send us directly by email or phone, and our replies.
  • Technical data collected automatically by our web host as a normal part of running a website — things like your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, kept in standard server logs.

We do not collect payment card details through this website — we don't currently take payments online, and quotes and invoicing are handled directly with you outside the site.

3. How our contact forms actually work

The consultation and contact forms on this site are handled by a third-party form processing service, FormSubmit, which relays what you submit — including any photos you attach — directly to our inbox by email. We don't run our own server or database to receive it; FormSubmit acts as the delivery mechanism between the form and our email. Once it arrives in our inbox, it's stored the same way any email sent to us would be.

4. Our legal basis for processing your data

We rely on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR, depending on the situation:

  • Consent — when you voluntarily submit a form or email us with your details.
  • Legitimate interests — responding to enquiries, keeping records of conversations, and running our business efficiently, in ways you'd reasonably expect and which don't override your rights.
  • Contract — if you go ahead with a restoration or detailing project, to communicate with you about and deliver that work.

5. Why we use your data

We use the personal data we hold to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotes or consultations.
  • Communicate with you about a project you've booked with us.
  • Keep basic records of past and ongoing work for our own accounting and reference.
  • Understand and improve how our site is used, via standard, non-identifying server logs.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we don't currently run marketing emails or newsletters. If that changes, we'll only email you for marketing purposes with your consent, and this policy will be updated first.

6. Cookies and third-party embeds

This website does not use cookies for advertising or analytics tracking. It does load a small number of resources from third parties to work properly:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — used to display the site's typefaces. This loads on every page and sends your device's IP address to Google, but does not set any cookies.
  • Google Maps — shows our location in the footer. Unlike the fonts, the map is not loaded automatically: you'll see a placeholder with a cookie notice, and the map (along with any cookies Google sets for it) only loads if you choose to accept it or click to load the map directly. If you decline, or don't choose either way, the map stays as a placeholder and no Maps cookie is set.

We don't control what these third parties do with that data beyond what's described in their own privacy policies (see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy).

7. Who we share data with

We don't sell or trade your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Our email provider, to send and receive messages with you.
  • FormSubmit, our form processing provider, which relays consultation and contact form submissions — including any photos attached — from this site to our inbox.
  • Our website hosting provider, which stores the site's files and standard access logs.
  • Google, in the limited way described in Section 6 (fonts and maps).
  • Law enforcement or regulators, but only where we're legally required to.

We don't currently use any analytics, advertising, or CRM tools — if we introduce any in future, we'll update this section first.

8. International data transfers

Google, which provides the fonts and maps embedded on this site, is a US-based company. Where your data is transferred outside the UK or European Economic Area as a result, Google relies on its own safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect it — see Google's privacy policy for details. Our email and hosting providers may also process data outside the UK/EEA depending on where their servers are located; we only use providers that offer appropriate contractual safeguards for this.

9. How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry and project-related emails for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, deliver any work agreed, and maintain basic business records afterwards — typically no longer than a few years, unless we're required to keep something longer (for example, for tax or accounting purposes). If you ask us to delete your information and we have no legal or contractual reason to keep it, we will.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it (right of access).
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data (right to rectification).
  • Ask us to delete your data, where there's no good reason for us to keep it (right to erasure).
  • Ask us to restrict or object to how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Ask for your data in a portable format, where relevant.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we're relying on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@safetay.uk. We'll respond within one month, as required by law.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

11. Keeping your data secure

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the personal data we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's privacy

This website and our services are aimed at adults. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children, and if we become aware that we have, we'll delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as our tools or practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. We'd encourage you to check back occasionally.
