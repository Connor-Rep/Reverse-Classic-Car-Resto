How We Work

What happens when you get in touch about your car, and what to send us so we can help.

Step By Step

  1. Get in touch

    Use the enquiry form, call us on +44 7792 372717 or email info@reverseclassic.co.uk. If you have photos of the car, you can attach them to the form.

  2. Free consultation

    We get back to you to talk through your car and what's involved. The consultation is free.

  3. The work happens in stages

    Restoration is organised into three stages. Each has its own page if you'd like to read more:

    • Mechanical
    • Body & Paint
    • Final Finish

What To Send Us

Not sure what to include in your message? These details help us understand your car before we talk:

  • The make, model and year
  • Photos from several angles
  • What you'd like done
  • Where the car is

Don't worry if you only have some of this. Send what you've got and we can talk through the rest.
