Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about working with us. If yours isn't here, just ask.

Questions

Where are you based, and what area do you cover?

Our workshop is in Ballintuim, near Blairgowrie in Perthshire. We look after cars across Perthshire, Dundee, Edinburgh and Glasgow and the surrounding areas, and north towards Aberdeen.

Do I have to pay for the first consultation?

No. The consultation is free. Send us your details through the enquiry form and we'll get back to you to talk through what's involved.

How do I get started?

Use the enquiry form, call us on +44 7792 372717 or email info@reverseclassic.co.uk. There's more on what happens next on our How We Work page.

Can I send photos of my car?

Yes. You can attach photos to the enquiry form, up to 10 photos and 10MB in total. JPG or PNG work best.

Can I ask a question before booking anything?

Yes. Call us or email, or put your question in the enquiry form, and we'll get back to you.

What kinds of work do you do?

Our restoration work is organised into three stages: Mechanical (engines, drivetrains and suspension), Body & Paint (panel fabrication, lead loading and paint) and Final Finish (interior re-trimming, paint correction and ceramic protection). You can see examples on our projects page.

Do you offer detailing, paint correction and ceramic protection?

Yes. Detailing, paint correction and ceramic protection are part of our Final Finish stage.

How does pricing work?

Every car is different, so we talk it through with you. Get in touch and tell us about your car and what you'd like done.

Do you work on kit cars?

We have an AC Cobra kit car on our projects page. If you have a kit car you'd like to ask about, get in touch and send some photos.
