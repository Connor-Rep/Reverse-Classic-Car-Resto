Polish & Protection

The finishing touch: Master-level detailing and long-term preservation for automotive art.

The Master's Finish

Multi-Stage Paint Correction

A restoration is only as good as its final surface. We perform exhaustive multi-stage machine polishing to remove microscopic imperfections, reaching levels of depth and clarity that exceed factory-new standards. Every inch of the bodywork is leveled and refined to a mirror finish.

Interior Preservation & Recovery

We restore interiors using period-correct materials, from Connolly leather to specialized wool carpets. Where original components can be preserved, we utilize advanced deep-cleaning and conditioning techniques to maintain historical integrity while ensuring a luxurious, like-new experience.

Premium Ceramic Protection

To preserve our hard work, we apply professional-grade ceramic coatings. These nanotechnology-based systems bond to the paintwork, providing extreme UV resistance, chemical durability, and a permanent hydrophobic layer that keeps your classic looking pristine for years to come.
