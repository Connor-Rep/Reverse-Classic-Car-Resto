Restoration Phase — Final Finish

The Last Ten Percent

This stage takes almost as long as everything before it, because it's what people actually see when the car rolls out. It's the difference between a finished rebuild and a car that stops people in their tracks.

  • Multi-Stage Paint Correction

    We correct the paint in stages until it holds a clean reflection, working out the swirl marks and haze a single pass would leave behind.

  • Interior Preservation & Recovery

    We condition and save original leather and trim wherever we can. When something's too far gone, we replace it using the same period-correct materials.

  • Premium Ceramic Protection

    A ceramic coat goes on last. It's more work up front than wax, but it holds up for years instead of months.
