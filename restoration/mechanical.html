Mechanical Restoration

The part of a restoration nobody sees, and the part that matters most.

Restoration Phase — Mechanical

Built To Run Right

Plenty of restorations look right and drive rough. We rebuild every mechanical system so the car actually goes, stops and handles the way it was designed to, not just starts up for the photos.

  • Engine Blueprinting

    The engine gets blueprinted to factory tolerances before it goes back in: block machined, every component matched and measured, nothing guessed at.

  • Drivetrain & Transmission

    Gearboxes, differentials and final drives come apart completely, and we replace bearings, seals and synchros based on what we actually find, not a fixed checklist.

  • Suspension & Braking

    We set the suspension geometry back to spec with period-correct bushings and dampers, and rebuild the brakes for how the car will actually be driven.
