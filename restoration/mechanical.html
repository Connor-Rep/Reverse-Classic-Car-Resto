Mechanical Restoration

Precision engineering and period-correct mechanical renewal — the heart of every restoration.

Built To Run Right

A car that looks the part but hesitates under load isn't finished. Every mechanical restoration ends with a vehicle that drives the way it was designed to, not just one that starts.

  • precision_manufacturing

    Engine Blueprinting

    Every engine is blueprinted to factory tolerances before it goes back in the bay — block machined, components matched, nothing left to guesswork.

  • settings

    Drivetrain & Transmission

    Gearboxes, differentials and final drives come apart completely. Bearings, seals and synchros are replaced on condition, not on a schedule.

  • speed

    Suspension & Braking

    Suspension geometry is set back to spec with period-correct bushings and dampers. Brakes are rebuilt for how the car actually gets driven.
