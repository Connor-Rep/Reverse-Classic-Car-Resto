Bodywork & Respray

Perfecting the lines of history: Artisan coachwork and period-correct paint finishes.

Artisan Craftsmanship

Panel Fabrication & Lead Loading

We reject modern plastic fillers. Our craftsmen utilize traditional panel beating and lead loading techniques to ensure seamless metal transitions and long-term structural integrity. Every curve and line is painstakingly restored to original factory profiles.

Period-Correct Paint Matching

Utilizing advanced spectroscopy and historical color archives, we replicate original factory finishes. Whether it's a deep nitrocellulose lacquer effect or a modern, high-durability equivalent, our paintwork is flawless and era-appropriate.

Rust Remediation & Protection

Restoration begins with a clean slate. We perform comprehensive rust removal via specialized blasting media that preserves original metal. Finished bodywork is treated with modern anti-corrosion systems for lifetime preservation.
