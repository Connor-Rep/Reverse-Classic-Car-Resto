Restoration Phase — Body & Paint

Every Panel, By Hand

Paint only ever looks as good as what's underneath it. Before a single coat goes on, every panel is put right the way the factory built it — not the way it's quickest to fix.

  • construction

    Panel Fabrication & Lead Loading

    Filler doesn't get near these panels. Metal is beaten and lead-loaded back to its original profile, the way it was built before plastic fillers existed.

  • palette

    Period-Correct Paint Matching

    Colour is matched against factory archives and period samples, then applied in lacquer or a modern equivalent — whichever suits the car.

  • security

    Rust Remediation & Protection

    Corrosion is media-blasted back to clean steel, not skimmed over. Modern anti-rust treatment goes on before a single coat of primer.
