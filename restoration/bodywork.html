Restoration Phase — Body & Paint

Every Panel, By Hand

A respray only looks as good as the metal underneath it, so that's where we start. Every panel gets straightened and put right before any paint goes near it.

  • Panel Fabrication & Lead Loading

    We beat and lead-load panels back to their original profile rather than reach for filler. It takes longer, but it's how these cars were actually built.

  • Period-Correct Paint Matching

    We match colour against factory archives and period samples, then spray in lacquer or a modern equivalent depending on what suits the car.

  • Rust Remediation & Protection

    Corrosion gets media-blasted back to clean steel rather than skimmed over, then treated properly before any primer goes on.
